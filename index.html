    Push Notifications
    Notify when app is in background
    Push notifications let LightChat ping you when someone messages or calls you and the app is in the background. Works on Android and on iPhone when LightChat is added to your Home Screen from Safari. Not supported in Trust Wallet's browser on iPhone.
    📲 Get LightChat on your phone
    Recommended: In Chrome, open lightchat.chat → menu → Add to Home screen / Install. No APK needed.
    Optional APK download
    Android + Chrome. Uninstall any old LightChat first. If the APK fails, use Add to Home screen instead.
    🔒 Privacy & messages
    Chats are not end-to-end encrypted. Messages, photos, and files are stored on LightChat servers so delivery and multi-device history work. On-chain memories use the blockchain and are permanent. Sign in with a wallet on Lightchain (chain 9200) only. Other networks are not allowed.
    ♻️ Chat history
    History is saved by default (Discord-style). If deleted posts stay hidden after a refresh, you can clear local delete memory. This does not undelete server posts.
    Advanced · P2P only
    Not recommended. Skips saving Gen Chat to the host. Posts may vanish after log off / refresh / another device. Leave off for Discord-like history (required for lightchain.ai handoff).
    📸 New Memory
    Where to save?
    ⏳ Cloud memories delete automatically after 90 days. If you want this memory saved forever, choose On-Chain.
    ⛓️ On-chain memories are permanent and can never be deleted. A small LCAI gas fee is required. Your Trust Wallet will ask you to confirm.
